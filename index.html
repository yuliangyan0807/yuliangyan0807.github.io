Yuliang Yan

闫宇量 | PhD Student in Artificial Intelligence

The Hong Kong University of Science and Technology (Guangzhou)

I am currently a second-year Ph.D. student in Artificial Intelligence at The Hong Kong University of Science and Technology (Guangzhou), supervised by Prof. Enyan Dai. My research focuses on AI for Protein, with the goal of building efficient and robust deep learning models to advance protein engineering. I am also dedicated to developing algorithms with rigorous mathematical guarantees for de novo drug design, such as molecular glues and peptides. In addition, my work extends to the development of trustworthy large language models.

Previously, I earned my master’s degree from the Fudan NLP Group at Fudan University (supervised by Prof. Xiaoqing Zheng), and my bachelor’s degree in Mathematics and Applied Mathematics from Shanghai University.
