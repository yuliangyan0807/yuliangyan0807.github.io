I am currently a second-year Ph.D. student in Artificial Intelligence at [The Hong Kong University of Science and Technology (Guangzhou)](https://www.hkust-gz.edu.cn/), supervised by Prof. [Enyan Dai](https://enyandai.github.io/). My research focuses on **AI for Protein**, with the goal of building efficient and robust deep learning models to advance protein engineering. I am also dedicated to developing algorithms with rigorous mathematical guarantees for *de novo* drug design, such as molecular glues and peptides. In addition, my work extends to the development of trustworthy large language models.

Previously, I earned my master's degree from the Fudan NLP Group at [Fudan University](https://www.fudan.edu.cn/) (supervised by Prof. Xiaoqing Zheng), and my bachelor's degree in Mathematics and Applied Mathematics from [Shanghai University](https://www.shu.edu.cn/).
